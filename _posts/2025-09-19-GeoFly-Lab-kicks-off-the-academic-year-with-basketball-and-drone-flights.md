To celebrate the start of the new academic year, GeoFly Lab gathered for an informal lab activity day that combined two of our favorite things: **team sports** and **field practice**.

We started with a friendly **basketball game**, a simple way to reconnect after the summer and welcome new members into the group. Beyond the fun, it was a reminder that good research teams are built not only through projects and publications, but also through shared time, trust, and collaboration.

<img src="/posts/images/2025091901.jpg" alt="GeoFly Lab basketball and team activity" style="width:100%; height:auto;" />

After the game, we transitioned outdoors for a short **drone flight session**. These hands-on activities help students build confidence in safe operations, mission planning, and data collection workflows—skills that directly support our research and education initiatives in GIS, remote sensing, and UAV-based mapping.

<img src="/posts/images/2025091902.jpg" alt="GeoFly Lab drone flight session" style="width:100%; height:auto;" />

We used the time to review pre-flight checklists, discuss flight safety and situational awareness, and reflect on how field protocols connect to downstream processing and reproducible analysis.

<img src="/posts/images/2025091903.jpg" alt="Students and lab members preparing for UAV operations" style="width:100%; height:auto;" />

As the quarter begins, we are excited to continue building an inclusive, supportive lab environment—one that values rigorous methods, open learning, and practical experience.
