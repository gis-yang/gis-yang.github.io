GeoFly Lab

Geospatial research and education at UC Santa Cruz

GeoFly Lab, in the Department of Environmental Studies at the University of California, Santa Cruz, studies environmental change using GIS, remote sensing, and UAV (drone) mapping. Our work spans wildfire, coastal seagrass ecosystems, and urban environments, and we train students in drone-based field methods and reproducible geospatial analysis.

Our research is supported by the National Science Foundation, NASA, and CAL FIRE.

Research areas

Wildfire Remote Sensing

Thermal, LiDAR, and multispectral drone mapping of prescribed burns, fire behavior, and fuels.

Coastal Seagrass Mapping

UAV and AI monitoring of eelgrass habitat, climate stress, and disease along the Pacific coast.

Urban-Environmental Data Science

Spatial statistics and remote sensing for urban heat, transportation, and crime.

Geospatial Education

Drone mapping training, K-12 outreach, and student research in GIS and remote sensing.
